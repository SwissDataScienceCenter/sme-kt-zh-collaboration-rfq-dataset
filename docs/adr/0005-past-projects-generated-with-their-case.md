# Past projects are generated with the Case that needs them, then frozen

The Company world core (organisation, rates, catalogue, manuals, Norm register) is built before any Case, as ADR 0001 requires. Past projects are the exception: each Case gets one or two deliberately similar past projects, generated together with that Case and then frozen into the Company world. Building all past projects up front would mean guessing which near neighbours future Cases need; generating them later keeps each similarity deliberate, at the cost of a Company world that grows over time.
