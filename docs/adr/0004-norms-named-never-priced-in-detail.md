# Norms are named and summarised, never priced in detail

SIA, SN EN and ISO norms appear only by number with a short description in our own words; no norm text is reproduced, and no Offer's price may depend on detailed norm content. Norm texts are copyrighted, and Cases that require expert norm interpretation would make the correct Offer impossible to verify. Swiss federal law (for example the Code of Obligations and the VAT Act) is public domain under Art. 5 URG and may be quoted verbatim.
