# Fictional company within the Swiss legal and business framework

The company, its customers, staff, prices and projects are invented, and no data from cohort SMEs enters the dataset; their interview needs only shape which problems appear. All documents follow Swiss practice (CHF, Swiss VAT, Swiss contract law, Swiss industry conventions) so cohort participants recognise their own day to day work. This allows open publication, at the cost of never matching any single SME exactly.
