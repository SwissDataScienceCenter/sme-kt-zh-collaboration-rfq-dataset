# Design log

Every design decision behind the dataset, in one place, separate from the Company world documents.
ADRs (`docs/adr/`) hold the few decisions that are hard to reverse; this log holds all decisions, small or large, and points to ADRs where they exist.

Fields: **ID**, **Date**, **Decision**, **Why**, **Source** (Maintainer = stated by the dataset maintainer; Sample = derived from a private sample; Proposal = proposed by Claude and accepted by the maintainer; Claude = taken by Claude during building, open for review), **Affects** (files or folders).
Status of a decision changes only by a new entry that supersedes it ("Supersedes DL-xxx").
